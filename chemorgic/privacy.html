Chemorgic — Privacy policy

2026-09-06

Data on your device

Chemorgic by Rizk Corsight, LLC has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, including for children. Progress, settings and trial status are stored on your device. Label text is recognized locally; photos and text are not uploaded by the app. Images may be cached temporarily during scanning. Camera access is optional; you can paste text instead. Optional reminders use local notifications. You can revoke camera and notification permissions in device settings.

Purchases

The 3-day trial starts at first launch without a charge or subscription. Afterwards, full access is available through an optional one-time purchase. The app contacts Apple or Google for prices, purchase verification, purchases and restoration. The store handles payments under its privacy policy; we do not receive your payment card or billing details.

Contact

Local data retention, backups and removal follow your operating system’s rules; use its app storage controls to remove app data. If you email support, we receive your address and message and retain them as needed to answer; you can request deletion. This policy website is hosted by GitHub, which may process web requests under its privacy policy. Updated policies are published here with a date.
