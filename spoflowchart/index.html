Spoflowchart

Turn a recorded procedure into an editable chart. Review the transcript, correct the steps, and export the result for your own use.

Recording and transcription

Allow microphone access when you choose to record. Speech language resources may need to be installed before use. Transcription can make mistakes: review names, numbers, decisions, and step order before relying on a chart.

Export and sharing

Use the app’s export options to save a chart or document through your platform’s file or share interface. Check the exported result before sharing, particularly when it includes private recordings or procedures.

Purchases and help

The in-app store shows trial terms and the current localized one-time unlock price. Restore purchases with the account that made the purchase and an available store connection. For help, include the app version, platform, and steps to reproduce the issue; avoid sending confidential audio or documents.
