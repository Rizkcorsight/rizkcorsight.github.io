Spoflowchart foreground services

This native Android recording demonstrates user-initiated microphone recording continuing with an ongoing notification, followed by local audio transcription with a processing notification.

Open or download the video

Demonstration: Android API 36 emulator, source-equivalent version 1.0.0 (3) debug build with the speech model installed through Play Core local testing. The emulator microphone is silent; the app’s no-sound warning is visible. This demonstrates service behavior, not speech recognition accuracy or a completed chart. No audio or transcript is uploaded for transcription.

  • 0:00–0:32: microphone and notification permissions, start recording.
  • 0:33–1:15: recording continues outside the app with an ongoing notification.
  • 1:16–1:27: return to the app and stop recording.
  • 1:28–1:46: local transcription notification while the app is in the background and airplane mode is enabled.
