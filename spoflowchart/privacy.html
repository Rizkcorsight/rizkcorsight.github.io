Spoflowchart — Privacy Policy

Spoflowchart records audio and processes transcripts and charts on your device. It has no developer account system or developer-operated transcription backend.

Microphone and speech processing

Microphone access is requested for recording. On iOS, transcription uses Apple’s on-device speech frameworks; required language assets are obtained through Apple’s system services. On Android, final transcription uses a packaged Whisper model, while optional live text uses the device’s on-device speech recognizer. Model delivery and speech-language installation can require a network connection. The app does not send recordings to a developer-operated server for transcription.

Files, exports, and retention

Recordings, transcripts, and charts are kept in local app storage until removed through the app or your platform’s storage controls. Imports, exports, and sharing happen when you choose them through system interfaces. A file or share provider may transfer selected content off your device; its policies apply. Android system app-data backup is disabled for this app. Manage exported copies, device backups, and files held by other providers separately from the app.

Platform services and purchases

Apple StoreKit or Google Play handles the one-time unlock, localized price, payment, restoration, and refunds. Spoflowchart receives purchase state to determine access, not payment card or store account credentials. Android model delivery and device speech services, and Apple speech asset services, operate under their providers’ policies. See the Apple Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising and support

Spoflowchart does not include advertising or a developer-operated analytics service. If you contact support, we receive your email address and the content you send to answer your request. Please avoid sending confidential recordings or personal information unnecessarily. The app does not ask children for direct contact information as part of its recording or chart features.
