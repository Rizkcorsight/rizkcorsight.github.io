Foldcast — Privacy policy

2026-09-06

Foldcast, by Rizk Corsight, LLC, does not collect personal data, including from children. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Gameplay works offline.

Data on your device

Progress, practice results, settings, purchase access and trial dates stay on your device. App removal follows your operating system’s rules; secure trial records in Apple Keychain may remain after removal.

Purchases

You choose when to start a local 3-day trial. It creates no subscription or charge. Afterwards, an optional one-time purchase unlocks permanent access. Apple or Google processes payments. The app may contact the store to load prices, check purchases, buy or restore access. We do not receive your payment card or billing details.

Sharing and reminders

Sharing is optional through the system share sheet; the chosen recipient applies its own privacy practices. Optional reminders use local notifications.
