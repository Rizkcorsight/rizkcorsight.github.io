Shuny Shade — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026 · Rizk Corsight, LLC

Shuny Shade has no developer account system, advertising, or developer-operated analytics or tracking backend. Calculations, camera images, obstruction horizons, saved properties and spots, settings, and exported reports remain on your device unless you explicitly share or export a file. The connected services described below receive limited data needed to provide their functions.

Camera and sky scan

If you choose Sky Scan, camera frames are processed on the device and are not uploaded by Shuny Shade. The manual horizon editor remains available without camera access.

Location and maps

If you choose to use device location, Shuny Shade requests one-time approximate location access to calculate the sun path; it does not track location in the background. You may instead enter or place a location manually.

When you open an interactive map, the app requests the OpenStreetMap tiles needed for the visible map area from the OpenStreetMap Foundation. Those HTTPS requests necessarily reveal the requested map-tile area and ordinary network information such as IP address and the app identifier to that service. Shuny Shade does not use those requests for advertising or profiling and does not operate an intermediary server. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy.

Google Play Services for AR (ARCore)

This application runs on Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), which is provided by Google and governed by the Google Privacy Policy. Shuny Shade uses device motion and camera tracking for its optional guided scan. It does not use ARCore Cloud Anchors or the ARCore Geospatial API.

Google states that ARCore collects a signed-in Google account identifier or a device identifier, performance and diagnostic information, and ARCore API usage/app activity for analytics, diagnostics, and service improvement. Google states that this information is encrypted in transit and is not shared with third parties. See Google's ARCore data-safety disclosure and the Google Privacy Policy.

On-device ML Kit processing

Google ML Kit subject segmentation helps distinguish the foreground from the sky. Image processing stays on the device; Google Play services may download the model. Google states that ML Kit collects limited device and application information, installation/device identifiers, performance metrics, API configuration, input/output sizes, feature versions, event types, and error codes for diagnostics and usage analytics. Google states that this information is encrypted in transit and is not shared with third parties. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure.

Purchases

The three-day trial starts locally. If you choose to buy or restore the one-time unlock, Google Play handles the transaction, localized price, entitlement, refund, and payment information under Google's policies. Shuny Shade receives the purchase state needed to unlock the app; Rizk Corsight does not receive your payment card or Google account credentials.

Notifications, files, retention, and deletion

Optional reminders are scheduled locally. Imported and exported Shuny Shade files and Sun Report PDFs are handled only at your direction through Android's file and share interfaces. Saved app records remain on your device until you delete them, empty the in-app trash, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. Data handled by Google or the OpenStreetMap Foundation is retained under their respective policies.

Children

Shuny Shade is a general-audience planning utility. It does not knowingly request a name, email address, account, or other direct contact information from children.

Security and contact

Connected requests use encrypted HTTPS connections. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed perfectly secure.
