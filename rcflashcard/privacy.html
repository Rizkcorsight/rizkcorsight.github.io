RC Flash Card — Privacy Policy

RC Flash Card processes imported study documents, extracted text, cards, and review history on your device. It has no developer account system, advertising, or developer-operated analytics backend.

Documents and images

You choose which files or images to import. Text recognition runs on your device using Apple system frameworks on iOS and bundled Google ML Kit recognition models on Android. Importing a document from a URL contacts that website, which receives the requested URL and ordinary network information such as your IP address under its own policies.

Android ML Kit

ML Kit processes recognition images locally. Separately, Google describes collection of device and app information, installation identifiers, and operational and usage metrics by the SDK. These metrics are distinct from the image and recognized text. See Google’s ML Kit data disclosure and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Files, backups, and sharing

The study library is stored locally. If you choose an external backup folder, the app writes library snapshots to that destination. A selected file provider may store these files remotely. Imported originals, extracted text, images, cards, and review records may be included in a backup. You control the destination and any later sharing; the file provider’s permissions, security, and retention policies apply. This release does not provide automatic two-way cloud sync.

Purchases

Apple StoreKit or Google Play handles purchases, restoration, payment details, and refunds. The app uses purchase state to determine access. Rizk Corsight does not receive your payment card or store account credentials. See the applicable Apple or Google privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Imported originals and library data may remain in local app storage after individual cards are removed. Remove the app’s stored data using your platform’s controls to clear that local copy. Backups, exports, shared files, and copies held by file providers must be deleted separately. Platform purchase records and SDK operational data follow the platform provider’s retention policies.

Support

If you email support, we receive the address and information you choose to send and use it to answer your request. Do not send sensitive study documents unnecessarily. RC Flash Card does not ask children for direct contact information as part of its study features.
