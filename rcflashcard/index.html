Your first study session

Import a document, review the proposed cards against the original source, and keep or correct the material you want to study. Extracted text and drafted cards can contain errors, especially with scans, complex layouts, or unfamiliar symbols.

Backups and recovery

Choose a backup folder you control and keep an exported copy before moving devices. Keep access to the same file provider when restoring. A file provider may store files in the cloud; its availability and privacy settings apply.

Purchases

Use the in-app purchase screen for current trial terms and the store’s localized one-time unlock price. Restore purchases using the store account that made the purchase. Purchase and restoration require a working store connection.

Get help

Include your platform, app version, and the steps that caused the problem. Please omit private study documents and personal information unless needed and intentionally shared.
