AIRONY Find privacy

Photos and analysis

Photos you choose for wrinkle or care-label analysis are processed on your device. AIRONY does not upload those photos to an analysis service. Analysis requires a supported device with an available on-device model. Model availability depends on the operating system and device; not every device supports analysis.

Android model diagnostics

On Android, AIRONY uses Google ML Kit and AICore for local AI processing. Google documents that the ML Kit SDK collects technical metrics for diagnostics and usage analytics: device and app information, identifiers, performance measurements, API configuration, input and output sizes, feature events and errors, and configured languages. Google states that these metrics are encrypted in transit using HTTPS and are not transferred to third parties. This metrics collection is separate from processing your photos on the device. See Google’s ML Kit data disclosure for details.

Saved records and backups

Saved photos, results, wardrobe records and preferences are stored on your device. Exporting a backup or sharing a result sends that copy to the destination you choose. Copies held by another app, recipient or device backup remain subject to that destination’s controls. You can delete saved records in AIRONY. Removing the app may not remove device backups, shared copies or secure access records.

Access and purchases

The current app stores trial/access information locally, including secure device storage. Apple or Google handles purchases and restore requests. The trial is started explicitly and lasts 72 hours. AIRONY uses native store ownership checks for the one-time unlock; it does not use a server account for analysis access. Do not send payment details to support.
